PROJECT 01

Digital Health Patient Portal QA

CompanyCVS Health
RoleQA Automation Engineer
PeriodMarch 2025 – Present
Digital Health Platform · Web · Mobile · API · Data Validation
Project overview

Supported quality assurance for a pharmacy digital health platform that enables patients to manage prescription-related activities, appointments, profile information, notifications, and other digital healthcare interactions.

The platform required reliable experiences across multiple application layers because a failure in one layer could affect patient access, healthcare workflow completion, data accuracy, or downstream pharmacy operations.

Quality objective

Ensure patients could safely complete high-priority digital health journeys while confirming that every action created accurate and consistent records across the application, service, and data layers.

Key work delivered
  • Developed a risk-based QA strategy focused on patient-facing workflows with the highest impact on access, engagement, and care coordination.
  • Validated secure patient login, account recovery, profile updates, appointment selection, prescription refill requests, notification preferences, and error-handling scenarios.
  • Tested complete business workflows from patient action through backend processing and record creation to confirm expected outcomes at every stage.
  • Evaluated functional behavior across web, mobile, APIs, integrations, and database records rather than testing each layer in isolation.
  • Performed regression, smoke, integration, exploratory, accessibility, performance, and release-readiness testing.
  • Documented defects with clear reproduction steps, expected versus actual behavior, supporting evidence, business impact, and severity to support efficient triage.
  • Partnered with product, engineering, and design teams to clarify requirements, identify risks early, and validate fixes before production release.
Accuracy and quality validation
  • Verified that prescription refill requests reflected the correct patient, medication details, request status, and workflow outcome across connected systems.
  • Confirmed appointment selections, cancellations, and updates were accurately reflected in both the patient experience and backend records.
  • Validated that profile and communication-preference updates persisted correctly and did not create conflicting patient information.
  • Tested negative and edge-case scenarios, including invalid inputs, incomplete information, expired sessions, authorization failures, duplicate requests, and service interruptions.
  • Checked that user-facing messages, API responses, and stored records remained consistent when a workflow succeeded, failed, or required additional action.
Evidence produced
  • Risk-based test strategy and release test plan.
  • End-to-end test scenarios for critical patient journeys.
  • API and data-validation checklists.
  • Regression and smoke-test execution results.
  • Defect reports with traceable evidence.
  • Accessibility and release-readiness validation records.
  • Quality status reporting for release decisions.
Measured result
  • Increased automated and regression test coverage by 40%.
  • Reduced regression execution time by 35%.
  • Strengthened release confidence by expanding validation across user interface, service, integration, and data layers.
PROJECT 02

Clinical Healthcare Application QA

CompanyCognizant — Client: AstraZeneca
RoleQA Engineer
PeriodNovember 2023 – December 2024
Clinical Healthcare Applications · Web · Mobile · API · Integration
Project overview

Delivered quality assurance for clinical healthcare applications supporting business and operational workflows across web, mobile, backend services, enterprise integrations, and clinical data exchanges.

The project required thorough validation because healthcare workflows depend on accurate data, reliable system integration, controlled user access, and clear release evidence.

Quality objective

Validate that clinical application workflows operated correctly across connected systems, clinical data exchanges remained accurate, and releases met functional, integration, and user-acceptance expectations.

Key work delivered
  • Translated requirements and acceptance criteria into functional, regression, integration, end-to-end, and user-acceptance test coverage.
  • Designed test scenarios for business-critical workflows across web, mobile, service, and integration layers.
  • Validated application behavior during normal usage, invalid input, system errors, missing data, service unavailability, and integration failures.
  • Supported release readiness through smoke testing, regression testing, exploratory testing, defect verification, and evidence collection.
  • Investigated defects by reproducing failures, isolating impacted workflows, gathering logs and request-response evidence, and documenting business impact.
  • Worked closely with cross-functional delivery teams to clarify expected behavior, prioritize defects, retest fixes, and maintain quality visibility throughout the release cycle.
  • Identified test-coverage gaps and recommended improvements to strengthen validation of high-risk workflows.
Accuracy and quality validation
  • Validated that clinical and operational data passed correctly between the user interface, backend services, and integrated enterprise systems.
  • Confirmed API responses matched expected business rules, error behavior, status handling, and data formats.
  • Checked that records created or updated through the application were accurately reflected in downstream systems.
  • Verified data consistency for create, update, search, status-change, and error-recovery workflows.
  • Tested authorization and access scenarios to ensure users could view and perform only the actions permitted for their role.
  • Confirmed that defects were traceable to requirements, reproducible by development teams, and properly retested after resolution.
Evidence produced
  • Requirements-based test scenarios and test-case repository.
  • Functional, regression, integration, and UAT execution evidence.
  • API validation records and response-analysis documentation.
  • Defect reports with reproduction steps, severity, impact, and retest status.
  • Test summary reports and release-readiness updates.
  • Coverage-gap analysis and improvement recommendations.
Quality outcome
  • Improved visibility into release risk by maintaining clear coverage across functional, integration, and end-to-end workflows.
  • Helped reduce the likelihood of post-release defects by identifying issues before release and ensuring fixes were validated across affected workflows.
  • Strengthened confidence in clinical data exchange by validating consistency across user-facing, backend, and integrated systems.

Enterprise ERP and Financial Workflow QA

CompanyCapgemini
RoleQA Analyst
PeriodMay 2021 – April 2023
SAP S/4HANA · Oracle ERP · Financial Services · Integration · UAT
Project overview

Supported quality assurance for enterprise ERP and financial-services workflows involving order processing, procurement, invoicing, supplier management, approvals, financial postings, integrations, and compliance-sensitive data.

The project involved interconnected business processes where one incorrect value, approval, interface message, or access-control issue could affect financial reporting, operational delivery, audit records, or downstream systems.

Quality objective

Ensure critical enterprise workflows processed accurately from initiation through completion, including correct calculations, approval routing, integration behavior, financial records, and role-based access.

Key work delivered
  • Created and executed functional, regression, integration, and end-to-end test cases for enterprise business workflows.
  • Validated Order-to-Cash processes, including customer data, sales orders, order changes, invoicing, payment-related workflows, and financial postings.
  • Validated Procure-to-Pay processes, including supplier onboarding, purchase orders, approval workflows, goods receipt, invoice processing, and payment-related data.
  • Tested business rules, approval limits, exception paths, error messages, duplicate entries, incomplete data, and status transitions.
  • Performed data validation across application screens, interfaces, ETL processes, and backend records.
  • Supported user-acceptance testing by preparing scenarios, tracking issues, maintaining test evidence, and assisting with sign-off readiness.
  • Participated in defect triage, root-cause discussions, fix validation, regression testing, and test-status reporting.
  • Coordinated with global development and business teams within Agile delivery cycles.
Accuracy and quality validation
  • Verified that customer, supplier, product, purchase-order, and sales-order information remained accurate throughout connected workflows.
  • Confirmed invoice values, taxes, quantities, discounts, approval statuses, and financial postings matched expected business rules.
  • Validated that data transferred correctly between ERP modules and connected systems without duplication, loss, incorrect mapping, or status mismatch.
  • Tested role-based permissions, approval routing, and access controls to confirm users could perform only authorized actions.
  • Verified data masking and access-control behavior for compliance-sensitive records.
  • Tested high-volume and exception scenarios to identify performance, validation, and workflow risks before release.
Evidence produced
  • Order-to-Cash and Procure-to-Pay test scenarios.
  • Requirements traceability matrix.
  • Functional, integration, regression, and UAT execution reports.
  • Data-validation checklist for ERP and interface records.
  • Access-control and approval-workflow test evidence.
  • Defect-triage records and root-cause documentation.
  • UAT sign-off and release-readiness documentation.
Quality outcome
  • Increased confidence that financial and operational workflows produced accurate results across ERP modules and integrated systems.
  • Improved defect detection through end-to-end validation of business rules, approvals, calculations, and data movement.
  • Supported compliant release decisions with documented functional, data, access-control, and user-acceptance evidence.
